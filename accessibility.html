Accessibility Statement

NFFE Local 1650 is committed to providing website content that is usable by everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our Accessibility Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA and to follow applicable Section 508 accessibility practices.

Our accessibility work includes keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, meaningful page structure, text alternatives, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.

Report an Accessibility Problem

If you cannot access any content or feature, email unioncomms1650@gmail.com. Please identify the page and describe the problem. We will work to provide the information in an accessible format.

Documents and External Services

  • Any membership form added to this site should be provided as an accessible, properly tagged document.
  • Some links open services managed by third parties. Contact us if a third-party service creates an accessibility barrier.
  • Alternative formats are available upon request by emailing the address above.
