Join NFFE Local 1650

Build a stronger workplace through collective representation, clear communication, and a shared voice.

Why Join?

Membership strengthens the Local’s ability to represent employees, address workplace concerns, and advocate for consistent application of the Master Agreement.

Workplace Representation

Get support navigating workplace concerns, representational questions, and contractual processes.

A Stronger Collective Voice

Help strengthen employee participation and advocacy on issues affecting working conditions.

Contract and Policy Support

Connect with stewards who can help identify applicable contract language, policy, and next steps.

Local Communication

Stay connected with Local 1650 information, resources, and opportunities to participate.

How to Join

  1. Download the membership form. Use the download button once the form is posted.
  2. Fill out the form. Complete all requested fields and sign where indicated.
  3. Email the completed form. Send it to unioncomms1650@gmail.com.
